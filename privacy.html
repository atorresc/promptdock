Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

The short version: PromptDock does not collect, transmit, store or sell any personal data. Everything you save stays in your own browser.

What the extension stores

Your prompt library — titles, prompt text, tags and a local usage counter — is saved using the browser's chrome.storage.local API. This data lives on your device. It is never uploaded, synchronised or transmitted to us or to anyone else.

You can export your entire library to a JSON file at any time, or delete all of it by removing the extension.

What the extension reads

PromptDock injects a prompt palette into supported AI chat sites and writes your selected prompt text into the page's message box. It does not read, record or transmit your conversations, your messages, or any other page content.

Payments

Paid subscriptions are handled by ExtensionPay, which processes payments through Stripe. When you subscribe, ExtensionPay stores your email address and subscription status in order to unlock paid features. We never receive or store your payment card details.

  • ExtensionPay privacy policy
  • Stripe privacy policy

Analytics and tracking

None. The extension contains no analytics SDK, no telemetry and no advertising code.
